Reflecting on a Fantastic AWS Student Community Night!

I had an incredible time sharing my insights and learning from the best at the recent AWS Student Community Night.

As part of the first batch, I participated in a roundtable discussion with Jessica Gilmore, EdD from the Global AWS team. We talked a lot about what students and communities truly need to grow, gain hands-on experience, and bridge the gap between academia and the industry.

We also discussed the real barriers students face, such as building technical confidence and overcoming the fear of public speaking (something I’m working on too!).

The best part? Reconnecting with familiar faces and meeting fellow tech enthusiasts. Huge shoutout to my girls Mary Jean Navarro and Rodelene Joy Leonorio, LPT: it’s always a highlight seeing you both at these events!

Looking forward to more growth and cloud-focused conversations. 🚀
